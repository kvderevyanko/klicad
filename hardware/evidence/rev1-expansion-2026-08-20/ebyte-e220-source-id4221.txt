1.Product Overview


1.1 Product Introduction

E220- T series modules are a new generation of LoRa wireless modules. This series of UART modules is developed
based on SEMTECH high-performance RF chips , has multiple transmission modes, operates in the 400 MHz and 900
MHz bands , features LoRa spread spectrum technology, TTL level output, and is compatible with 3.3V I/O port voltage.
E220- T series modules adopt the latest generation of LoRa spread spectrum technology . LoRa™ direct sequence spread
spectrum technology offers advantages such as longer communication distance, strong anti-interference capabilities, and
extremely high security. Its maximum transmit power reaches 30dBm, and it features a PA power amplifier and an LNA
low-noise amplifier, thereby improving communication stability and extending communication distance. Industrial-grade
standards ensure its stability and consistency. Currently in stable mass production, it is widely used in industries such as
metering, IoT upgrades, and smart homes .
The following diagram shows eight modules with different power, frequency bands, and packages. It is only an
appearance illustration . For detailed parameters , please refer to the E220-T series product selection comparison
in section 2.1 .




1.2 Features and Functions

     It adopts a new generation of LoRa spread spectrum modulation technology, which has a longer communication
      distance and stronger anti-interference ability;
     Supports multiple frequency bands: 433 / 868/915MHz (customizable) ;
     Supports serial port firmware upgrades , making maintenance more convenient;
     It supports both HEX and AT command systems , allowing users to choose flexibly .
     It supports users setting their own communication keys, which cannot be read, greatly improving the confidentiality
      of user data;
     Supports RSSI signal strength indication function for evaluating signal quality;
     It supports over-the-air wake-up, which is an ultra-low power consumption function suitable for battery-powered
      applications.
     Supports fixed-point transmission and broadcast transmission;
     The air transmission rate is adjustable in multiple levels;

5.1.1.2 Fixed-point transmission for quick use (specifying the destination address and channel)

Data is sent and received using a fixed data format: target address + target channel + data , which effectively avoids some
interference.

mode (M1, M0 pins set to 1, 1), change the transparent transmission mode to fixed-point transmission, and finally write

5.3 Serial port firmware upgrade

    If the customer needs to upgrade the firmware, they need to find the corresponding BIN file provided by the official
website and then use the host computer provided by the official website to upgrade the firmware. Under normal
circumstances, users do not need to upgrade the firmware and should not use the "AT+IAP" command .
    The necessary pins for the upgrade must be brought out (M1, M0, AUX, TXD, RXD, VCC, GND). Then, send the
"AT+IAP" command in configuration mode to enter the upgrade mode. If you need to exit the IAP upgrade mode, you
need to keep the power on and wait for 60 seconds. The program will exit automatically. Otherwise, even if you restart, it
will enter the upgrade mode indefinitely.
    After entering upgrade mode, the baud rate will automatically switch to 115200 until it automatically exits, during
which time logs will be output.

7. AT Commands

    Parameter configuration or querying using AT commands must be done in configuration mode (the serial port in
      configuration mode is forcibly fixed to 9600 8N1);
    In software versions (7463-0-14) and earlier, a newline character is not required at the end of AT commands entered
      by the user.
    Software versions (7463-0-15) and later automatically recognize the carriage return and line feed character at the end
      of user AT commands, and will still recognize them even if a carriage return and line feed character is not added.
    An AT command recognition or execution error will result in the response " =ERR ".
    When the input parameters exceed the range, they will be restricted. Please do not allow the parameters to exceed the
      range to avoid unforeseen situations.

8.Hardware Design

     It is recommended to use a DC regulated power supply to power this module, with the power supply ripple


Copyright © 2012–2025 , Chengdu Ebyte Electronic Technology Co. , Ltd.                                                         46
Chengdu Ebyte Electronic Technology Co., Ltd.                                                      E220-T Series User Manual

      coefficient as small as possible, and the module must be reliably grounded;
     Please ensure the correct connection of the positive and negative power terminals. Reversing the connection may
      cause permanent damage to the module.
     Please check the power supply and ensure it is within the recommended voltage range. Exceeding the maximum
      value will cause permanent damage to the module.
     Please check the power supply stability; the voltage should not fluctuate significantly or frequently.
     When designing power supply circuits for modules, it is often recommended to retain a margin of more than 30% to
      ensure long-term stable operation of the entire unit.
     The module should be kept as far away as possible from power supplies, transformers, high-frequency traces, and
      other parts with high electromagnetic interference.
     High-frequency digital traces, high-frequency analog traces, and power traces must avoid the area under the module.
      If it is absolutely necessary to run under the module, assuming the module is soldered on the Top Layer, lay ground
      copper on the Top Layer of the module contact area (all copper and well grounded), and run the traces close to the
      digital part of the module on the Bottom Layer.
     Assuming the module is soldered or placed in the Top Layer, it is also wrong to arbitrarily route traces in the
      Bottom Layer or other layers, as this will affect the module's stray emissions and receiver sensitivity to varying
      degrees.
     If there are devices around the module that cause significant electromagnetic interference, it will greatly affect the
      module's performance. Depending on the intensity of the interference, it is recommended to keep them away from
      the module. If possible, appropriate isolation and shielding can be implemented.
     If there are traces around the module that cause significant electromagnetic interference (high-frequency digital,
      high-frequency analog, power supply traces), it will greatly affect the module's performance. Depending on the
      intensity of the interference, it is recommended to keep them away from the module. If possible, appropriate
      isolation and shielding can be implemented.
     The antenna mounting structure has a significant impact on module performance; it is essential to ensure that the
      antenna is exposed and preferably vertically upward.
     When the module is installed inside the housing, a high-quality antenna extension cable can be used to extend the
      antenna to the outside of the housing.
     Antennas should never be installed inside a metal casing, as this will greatly reduce the transmission distance.




9.Frequently Asked Questions


9.1 Transmission distance is not ideal

     When there are obstacles in line-of-sight communication, the communication distance will be reduced accordingly;
     Temperature, humidity, and co-channel interference can all lead to increased packet loss rates in communications.
     The ground absorbs and reflects radio waves, and the test results are poor when the ground is close to the ground.
     Seawater has a strong ability to absorb radio waves, so testing results are poor at the seaside.
     If there are metal objects near the antenna, or if it is placed inside a metal casing, the signal attenuation will be very
      severe.
     Power register settings are incorrect; air speed is set too high (the higher the air speed, the shorter the distance).


Copyright © 2012–2025 , Chengdu Ebyte Electronic Technology Co. , Ltd.                                                         47
Chengdu Ebyte Electronic Technology Co., Ltd.                                                     E220-T Series User Manual

     At room temperature, the power supply voltage is lower than the recommended value; the lower the voltage, the
      lower the power output.
     The antenna may be poorly matched with the module or have quality issues.




9.2 Module is easily damaged

     Please check the power supply and ensure it is within the recommended voltage range. Exceeding the maximum
      value will cause permanent damage to the module.
     Please check the power supply stability; the voltage should not fluctuate significantly or frequently.
     Please ensure anti-static operation during installation and use, as high-frequency devices are sensitive to
      electrostatic discharge.
     Please ensure that the humidity is not too high during installation and use, as some components are
      humidity-sensitive.
     Unless there are special requirements, it is not recommended to use it at excessively high or low temperatures.


9.3 The bit error rate is too high.

     If there is interference from a co-channel signal nearby, move away from the source of interference or change the
      frequency or channel to avoid the interference.
     An inadequate power supply can also cause garbled characters, so it is essential to ensure the reliability of the power
      supply.
     Poor quality or excessive length of extension lines or feeders can also lead to a high bit error rate.
