# Rev.1 pre-production audit — resumed after U3 DBV release

## Scope

The only physical exception to the metadata-only audit was the separately
reviewed U3 DBV0005A release. It is recorded under
\`hardware/evidence/rev1-u3-dbv-release-2026-08-21/\`. All later PCB changes
are limited to title/status, Dwgs.User, and F.Silk.

## Reused bounded evidence

The earlier footprint/DFM audit found no blocker for U1, U4, L1, the PTH
features, filled GND returns, or the ESP32 antenna rule area. U1 has a
1.680-mm EP and 1.550-mm paste aperture (85.9 %); this remains a first-article
stencil observation, not a current blocker. PTH minimum radial rings are:
Samtec 0.330 mm, JST/J6/J9/JP1 0.350 mm, and TP holes 0.500 mm. These are
ordinary low-cost-PCB, hand-repairable geometries.

U3 is no longer an audit blocker: TI SN74AHCT1G125 DBV0005A drawing
\`4214839/K\` (08/2024) is represented by 0.60 x 1.10 mm pads at released
TI centres. The U3 implementation reviewer verified the pin map, C7 local
bypass, J9 data path, and unrelated-copper immutability.

## Return paths and access

The filled board has no isolated GND island: the U1 F.Cu return joins global
B.Cu GND through vias at (68.700, 54.000) and (70.500, 53.000); the E220 local
return reaches the B.Cu main GND region. OLED routes are F.Cu and do not split
the B.Cu GND return. Native DRC verifies the antenna rule area is free.

J4, J8, JP1, ESP32 USB-C and removal envelope, E220 SMA/removal envelope,
J5, J6, J9, and TP1...TP5 are accessible on the bare PCB. Final enclosure
access, mounting, and battery strain relief are deliberate first-article
mechanical risks: mounting is deferred to enclosure/Rev.2 and strain relief
is enclosure/harness responsibility in Rev.1.

## Production-layer policy

The future fabrication export must be explicitly limited to F.Cu, B.Cu,
F.Mask, B.Mask, F.SilkS, B.SilkS only if used, Edge.Cuts, and drill files.
Dwgs.User, Cmts.User, F.Fab, F.CrtYd, and Margin are diagnostic-only and must
not be emitted as fabrication Gerbers. No production files are generated by
this audit.
