# Independent DFM observations for PCBWAVE 2608134199

- Standard rigid capability lists 6 mil track width/space, 8 mil pad space, 0.30 mm drill, 0.15 mm text stroke, and 0.80 mm text height. The baseline scratch DRC applies those limits and returns zero violations. The production project's DRC exclusions array is empty. The board contains 61 PTH component/probe holes, 58 through vias with 0.30 mm drill and 0.60 mm diameter, and three 3.20 mm NPTH mounting holes. The 0.15 mm via annular ring is a design measurement. [PCBWAVE standard capabilities](https://www.pcbwave.com/capabilities.html)
- J4/J8 JST top-entry two-circuit connectors have 1.00 mm holes at 2.50 mm pitch. JST gives ±0.05 mm tolerance on the holes and pitch, and notes the influence of laminate and drilling process. PCBWAVE publishes a general 0.10 mm PTH diameter tolerance. Request explicit confirmation of the finished hole tolerance and connector insertion on the first article. [JST p. 2, local rendering](jst-2.png)
- The 41 Samtec socket holes at J1/J2/J3/J5 use Ø1.04 mm and 2.54 mm pitch. JP1 uses the dedicated Samtec TSW footprint and two Ø1.02 mm holes. The previous 1.00 mm generic-header discrepancy has been corrected. U4 has the dedicated TI DCY footprint, a 5.80 mm opposing row pitch, 2.15 × 0.95 mm lead lands on 2.30 mm pitch and a 2.15 × 3.25 mm tab on AUX_3V3. Both of the earlier library mismatch warnings are absent in the native baseline with severity enabled. The pad dimensions and net map support the correction.
- U1 uses 16 peripheral lands and one copper EP tied to GND. The separate paste-only feature provides a 1.55 × 1.55 mm stencil aperture over a 1.68 × 1.68 mm EP (85.1% area). The absence of a drilled opening through this exposed surface eliminates a direct via-in-pad paste loss path. The fine-pitch artwork uses nominal zero mask expansion, while TI illustrates mask options with rounded land corners. Confirm black mask registration, solder-paste release, stencil thickness, HASL planarity and first-article QFN joint inspection. Electrical thermal capability still requires load validation. [TI RGT0016C](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62133.pdf)
- The 145 × 90 mm two-layer baseline contains 202 tracks, four copper zones and seven keepouts. Antenna footprint/routing/copper exclusion, protected buck cell and mounting geometry passed the project contract. First-article fit of the actual ESP32/E220/OLED modules, OLED body, enclosure and antenna range remain assembly-level validation.
- Baseline native PCB/schematic parity is distinct from geometric DRC. Thirteen KiCad generated unconnected-net names correspond to intended NC pads, three footprints correspond to board-only mounting holes H1–H3, and four field/attribute differences at J6/J9 merit metadata synchronization. The project electrical parity checker passes. Final counts must be reported against the implemented source.
